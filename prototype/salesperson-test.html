Qualification

Outcome (pause / lost / refusal)

A lost deal is contacted again only with a recorded change dated after the loss (an option attached, verified evidence recorded, a documented price note) or on the agreed revisit date. Without a loss date nothing can be dated after it. A pause is explicit state (readiness or stage); a leftover revisit date never re-engages a live deal.
